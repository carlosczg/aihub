# ADR-001 — AI Hub must remain independent from any AI model

## Status

Accepted

---

## Date

2026-07-12

---

## Context

The primary objective of AI Hub is to preserve and organize professional knowledge accumulated over many years.

The AI ecosystem evolves rapidly. New models, runtimes, frameworks, and providers appear frequently.

Building the platform around a single AI provider would create unnecessary vendor lock-in and reduce long-term maintainability.

---

## Decision

AI Hub will be designed as an independent knowledge platform.

Knowledge will exist independently from:

- Claude
- ChatGPT
- Codex
- Gemini
- Databricks
- AWS Bedrock
- Future AI providers

AI models are considered consumers of knowledge, not owners of knowledge.

---

## Consequences

Positive:

- Future-proof architecture.
- Easy migration between AI providers.
- Lower vendor lock-in.
- Stable knowledge lifecycle.
- Reusable knowledge assets.

Negative:

- Requires additional abstraction layers.
- Initial architecture is slightly more complex.
- Documentation becomes more important.

---

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative A

Build AI Hub directly around Claude.

Rejected because replacing Claude would require redesigning the platform.

---

### Alternative B

Build AI Hub directly around ChatGPT.

Rejected for the same reason.

---

### Alternative C

Build AI Hub around Omnigent.

Rejected because Omnigent is an execution runtime, not the knowledge platform itself.

---

### Selected Alternative

Build AI Hub as an independent Knowledge Platform.

Use Omnigent only as the execution runtime.

AI providers become interchangeable components.

---

## Long-Term Impact

This decision allows AI Hub to evolve independently from current AI technologies.

Future migrations should require changing only the runtime layer while preserving the knowledge platform and its assets.
